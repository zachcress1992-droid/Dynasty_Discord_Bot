Scouting Desk Update - Week 1 · Filed by K.O.

Y'all sleeping on Aston Jantay and I don't get it. Kid ran a 4.3 at a pro day I'm pretty sure I made up, has 'generational vision,' and I would take him over any running back in this class, this decade, possibly this century. Write it down.

Here's my concern, and I say this with love: I hear Dr. Fumbles is in on him. I've been right about this stuff before — everybody forgets that part — and I'll be right again. Jantay's fine. It's the situation that scares me. Some rookies need a real culture around them. Dr. Fumbles has a culture, alright, it's just the culture of a guy adding two tight ends after a bad Sunday. Book it.

The Week That Was: Blowouts, Bad Beats, and One Very Loud 103.59
The BeatStraight news, no spin

MattRhulesGLP-1 opened the year by dropping 211.47 on Dr. Fumbles, who mustered a measly 103.59 in response — a 107.88-point margin that's less 'fantasy football matchup' and more 'live sacrifice.' Dr. Fumbles now sits at 0-2 with the fewest points in the league.

Caleb Williams was the overall top scorer in the league with 41.26 points, single-handedly dragging The Wiggles past CrabCakes & Football 189.45-166.68. Josh Allen wasn't far behind with 39.66 for Daddy's Naughty Boys, who needed every one of those points to hold off I am Jeff, 197.29-174.43.

The cruelest result of the week belonged to jgann, who scored 218.98 — the second-highest total of any team, real or losing — and still fell to Dak Lives Matter's 244.23, powered by Derrick Henry's 35.05. jgann now sits 1-1 despite having the look of a juggernaut on paper.

There's Always Next Year got 32.52 from T.J. Watt and beat up on bigtuna1108, 187.66-144.39. Jameson and Consequences rode Trevor Lawrence's 34.1 to a 166.98-130.67 win over Just Yorkin' It, who along with bigtuna1108, CrabCakes, I am Jeff, and Dr. Fumbles now form fantasy's saddest support group at 0-2 (well, 0-2 for four of them anyway).

Dr. Fumbles Panic-Added His Way Into a Wikipedia Page for Bad Decisions
The Unqualified TakeBig theory, small evidence, no regrets

Let's talk about Dr. Fumbles, who got run over by MattRhulesGLP-1's 211.47 like a raccoon that didn't respect the crosswalk. A 107.88-point margin in Week 1 isn't a bad beat, it's a restraining order. And instead of sitting with his feelings, this man went to the waiver wire and added Mack Wilson AND Michael Mayer in the same week, dropping Kendrick Law and Jaylin Noel like they personally set his house on fire. Buddy, the problem wasn't your WR4. The problem is everything.

Meanwhile jgann needs to file a formal grievance with the fantasy gods. Scoring 218.98 — more than four other teams managed in wins — and still losing is the kind of thing that should come with a participation trophy and a complimentary bottle of whiskey. Jahmyr Gibbs went off for 32.35 and it didn't matter one bit. That's not bad luck, that's the universe personally trolling you.

No blockbuster trades to dissect this week (a shame, honestly, we love chaos), but shoutout to The Wiggles for quietly adding Samaje Perine like a guy stocking up on canned goods before a storm nobody else sees coming — smart, boring, effective, exactly the vibe of a 2-0 team riding a 41-point Caleb Williams game. And a tip of the cap to There's Always Next Year and Just Yorkin' It for cutting bait on Derrick Brown and Dante McClellan respectively — sometimes addition by subtraction is the whole strategy, especially when your subtraction was already subtracting from you.

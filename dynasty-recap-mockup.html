Scouting Desk Update · Week 14

Calloway's tape is still the best of any receiver in this class — the issue is everything around it. Ashford State benched him for the final two games of the regular season, officially "a coaching decision." Unofficially, campus police confirmed an open report tied to a leased vehicle registered to Calloway that was found 200 miles off campus with body damage no one in the program will explain.

None of it shows up in a box score. All of it shows up in a medical/character grade that three teams in our own league's front offices have already started arguing about. Skol Dorks' scouting notes (leaked, allegedly, by someone in that Discord) still have him as a top-three rookie target — which, given this front office's history of ignoring red flags for ceiling, is either bold or exactly on brand. Cook County has him crossed off the board entirely, which is rich coming from an organization that once traded a first for a kicker.

And now this: a gas station attendant two exits from the impound lot reported finding a duffel bag stuffed with what she described as "an unreasonable amount of cash" zip-tied to a shopping cart, abandoned, no note. Local police have declined to connect it to anything. We are absolutely connecting it to something. The morals of somebody in this league's front office come into question yet again.

Nothing here is confirmed. All of it is deeply, wonderfully stupid. That's exactly the point — for him, and for whoever drafts him in April.

The Trade That Tells You Everything About This League
The Simmons-ish TakeBig theory, small evidence, no regrets

Okay so I need you to walk with me on this one. The Kelce Types just traded DeVonta Smith for a 2027 first, and everyone in the group chat is calling it a punt. I don't think it's a punt. I think it's a Draft Day Fake-Out — you sell low on a name guy, stockpile picks, and then in April you're the team with three firsts pretending you didn't plan this the whole time. We've seen this movie. It never works the way the GM thinks it will, and I love that for them.

Meanwhile Zero Dark Thirty needed a one-point escape act against a team that's now openly rebuilding, which is its own little omen. You don't win a title by needing overtime heroics against a team tanking in Week 14. Bold prediction, and I stand by zero of my bold predictions historically: this trade ages like milk by Thanksgiving of next season.
